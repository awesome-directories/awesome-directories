## Planning Your Launch

A successful product launch requires careful planning and coordination across multiple channels. Directory submissions are essential, but they work best as part of a comprehensive strategy.

## The Launch Timeline

### 3 Months Before Launch

**Focus:** Build anticipation and collect early feedback

- Create landing page with email capture
- Submit to pre-launch directories (BetaList, Launching Next)
- Build social media presence
- Engage with target communities

### 1 Month Before Launch

**Focus:** Prepare launch materials and build momentum

- Finalize product description and visuals
- Create launch day content (blog posts, videos)
- Reach out to potential supporters
- Schedule social media posts

### Launch Week

**Focus:** Maximum visibility and engagement

- Submit to major directories (Product Hunt, Hacker News)
- Engage with comments and feedback
- Share updates on social media
- Send email to subscribers

### Post-Launch

**Focus:** Maintain momentum and iterate

- Submit to remaining directories
- Analyze metrics and feedback
- Iterate on product
- Plan content marketing

## Multi-Channel Approach

Combine directory submissions with:

### Content Marketing

- Blog posts targeting keywords
- Guest posting on relevant sites
- YouTube videos and tutorials
- Podcasts and interviews

### Social Media

- Twitter/X engagement
- LinkedIn thought leadership
- Facebook groups
- Reddit communities

### Email Marketing

- Pre-launch list building
- Launch announcements
- Regular newsletters
- Onboarding sequences

### Paid Advertising

- Google Ads for high-intent keywords
- Social media ads for awareness
- Retargeting campaigns
- Sponsored content

## Coordinating Your Efforts

**Week 1: Directory Blitz**
- Major directories (Product Hunt, Hacker News)
- High-DR directories
- Industry-specific directories

**Week 2-4: Sustained Submission**
- Secondary directories
- Niche communities
- Local directories (if applicable)

**Month 2+: Content & SEO**
- Regular blog posts
- Guest posting
- Link building
- Community engagement

## Measuring Success

Track KPIs across channels:

- **Traffic sources** and volume
- **Conversion rates** by channel
- **Customer acquisition cost** (CAC)
- **Time to first value**
- **Retention and churn**

## Common Launch Mistakes

Avoid these errors:

1. **Launching without validation** - Test with beta users first
2. **Spreading too thin** - Focus on 2-3 channels initially
3. **Ignoring feedback** - Listen and iterate quickly
4. **Burning out** - Pace yourself for the long run
5. **No follow-up** - Launch is just the beginning
