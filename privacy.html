Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 12, 2025

Our Commitment to Privacy

Awesome Directories is an open-source project that respects your privacy. We collect minimal data necessary to provide our service and never sell your personal information to third parties.

This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information. As an open-source project, we prioritize transparency and user control.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Authentication Data (via Supabase)

When you create an account using OAuth authentication, we collect:

  • Email address - From your Google or GitHub account
  • OAuth provider ID - Unique identifier from Google or GitHub
  • Display name/username - Public profile information from your OAuth provider
  • Profile picture - Avatar from your OAuth provider (if available)
  • Account creation date - Timestamp of when you first signed up

This data is securely stored and managed by Supabase, our backend infrastructure provider.

1.2 User-Generated Content

When you use our service, we store:

  • Directory submissions - Information you submit about new directories
  • Favorites - Directories you've marked as favorites
  • Votes - Your upvotes or downvotes on directories
  • Reviews & comments - Your written feedback about directories
  • Submission tracking - Record of your directory submissions and their status

1.3 Analytics Data (via Pirsch)

We use Pirsch Analytics, a privacy-first, cookie-free analytics service. Pirsch collects:

  • Page views - Which pages you visit
  • Referrer information - Where you came from
  • Browser & device type - General device information
  • Geographic location - Country-level location (not precise)
  • Session duration - How long you spend on the site

Important: Pirsch is fully GDPR-compliant and does NOT use cookies or track personal information. All analytics data is anonymized and aggregated. No IP addresses are stored.

1.4 Newsletter Data (via Mautic)

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect:

  • Email address - To send you updates and launch strategies
  • Subscription source - Where you signed up (e.g., footer, popup)
  • Subscription date - When you subscribed
  • Email engagement - Whether you open or click links in our emails (standard email marketing metrics)

Newsletter data is managed by Mautic, our email marketing provider. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data only for the following purposes:

  • Provide core features - Authentication, favorites, submissions, voting, and reviews
  • Improve the service - Understand usage patterns and optimize user experience
  • Send newsletters - Deliver launch strategies and new directory updates (opt-in only)
  • Prevent abuse - Detect and prevent spam, manipulation, or malicious activity
  • Communicate updates - Notify you of important changes to the service
  • Comply with legal obligations - Respond to valid legal requests if required

We do NOT: Sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for purposes unrelated to Awesome Directories.

3. Data Storage & Security

3.1 Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored with our third-party service providers:

  • Supabase - PostgreSQL database hosted on secure cloud infrastructure ( Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Pirsch - Analytics data stored in EU servers ( Pirsch Privacy Policy)
  • Mautic - Newsletter subscriber data on email marketing servers

3.2 Security Measures

We take reasonable security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption - All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS/SSL)
  • OAuth authentication - We never store passwords; authentication is handled by Google/GitHub
  • Database security - Supabase implements row-level security and encryption at rest
  • Access controls - Limited access to production data by maintainers
  • Regular updates - Dependencies and infrastructure are kept up to date

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data with:

4.1 Essential Service Providers

  • Supabase - Database and authentication infrastructure
  • Pirsch - Privacy-first analytics (anonymized data only)
  • Mautic - Newsletter delivery

4.2 Public Data

Some data is publicly visible by design:

  • Directory submissions - Approved submissions become part of the public dataset
  • Reviews & comments - Public feedback on directories
  • Vote counts - Aggregated voting data (individual votes may be anonymized)
  • Open source data - Directory database is publicly available on GitHub

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests (e.g., court orders, subpoenas).

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Good news: We use minimal cookies and tracking.

5.1 Essential Cookies

  • Authentication cookies - Supabase session tokens to keep you logged in
  • Local storage - Preferences and UI state (stored locally in your browser)

5.2 No Tracking Cookies

We do NOT use third-party advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or invasive analytics. Pirsch Analytics is completely cookie-free and does not track users across sites.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access - Request a copy of the data we have about you
  • Correction - Request corrections to inaccurate data
  • Deletion - Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability - Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-out - Unsubscribe from newsletters at any time
  • Revoke access - Disconnect OAuth access via your Google/GitHub settings

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@awesome-directories.com
  • GitHub Issues: Report privacy concerns

We will respond to your request within 30 days. For account deletion, some public contributions (like approved directory submissions) may remain in the public dataset but will be disassociated from your identity.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for the following periods:

  • Account data - Retained while your account is active
  • User-generated content - Retained indefinitely as part of the public dataset (can be anonymized upon request)
  • Analytics data - Retained for up to 24 months by Pirsch
  • Newsletter data - Retained until you unsubscribe
  • Deleted accounts - Permanently deleted within 90 days, except for legally required records

If you delete your account, we will remove your personal information but may retain anonymized or aggregated data for analytics and service improvement.

8. Children's Privacy

Awesome Directories is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

9. International Users

Awesome Directories is available globally. By using our service, you consent to the transfer and processing of your data by our service providers, which may be located in different jurisdictions.

Our primary infrastructure provider, Supabase, operates in compliance with GDPR and other international privacy regulations. We strive to protect your data regardless of where you are located.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

For significant changes, we will:

  • Post a notice on our homepage
  • Announce it in our newsletter
  • Document changes in our GitHub repository

Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated privacy policy.

11. Open Source Transparency

As an open-source project, we believe in transparency. You can:

  • Review our source code - See exactly how we handle data on GitHub
  • Audit our practices - Verify our privacy claims in the codebase
  • Contribute improvements - Submit PRs to enhance privacy features
  • Report concerns - Open issues for privacy-related questions

This level of transparency is only possible because we're open-source. We encourage security researchers and privacy advocates to review our code.

12. Contact Us About Privacy

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this privacy policy or your personal data, please contact us:

  • Privacy Email: privacy@awesome-directories.com
  • General Contact: contact@awesome-directories.com
  • GitHub Issues: awesome-directories/awesome-directories

We take privacy seriously and will respond to all inquiries promptly and professionally.

Privacy Policy Summary

TL;DR:

  • We collect minimal data (email, OAuth info, usage data) to provide our service
  • We use privacy-first analytics (Pirsch) with no cookies
  • We never sell your data or share it with advertisers
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion at any time
  • Our code is open-source, so you can verify our privacy practices
  • We're transparent, community-driven, and respect your privacy 🔒
